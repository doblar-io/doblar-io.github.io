DOBLAR.IO / ABOUT & CV

Mariano
Ulibarri.

Artist / DJ / Photographer / Designer / Educator. Las Vegas, New Mexico.

My work includes photography, graphic design, DJing, live visuals, and creative technology.

I founded Parachute Factory in Las Vegas, New Mexico and helped develop the Makerstate Initiative, bringing hands-on creative programs to libraries and communities around the state. My work has included collaborations with museums, educators, and artists.

I currently direct the Santa Fe Creative Coding Initiative at Santa Fe Community College. Outside of that role, doblar.io is my independent work in music, images, print, and electronic media.
