Mariano
Ulibarri.

Artist, DJ, photographer, designer, educator, and maker of things. Based in Las Vegas, New Mexico.

My work moves between music, photography, graphic design, and creative technology. I like handmade marks, found images, unexpected collisions, and the strange things that happen when different disciplines meet.

I've spent more than two decades DJing while also building spaces and opportunities for other people to create. I founded the Parachute Factory makerspace in Las Vegas, New Mexico, helped bring hands-on making to libraries across the state through the Makerstate Initiative, and worked at the intersection of media arts, computer science, museums, and community education.

Today I direct the Santa Fe Creative Coding Initiative at Santa Fe Community College. Outside of that work, doblar.io is where I follow other impulses: record selections, event posters, walks with a camera, projections, moving images, and experiments that don't always have a category.

A practice, not a fixed job description.
